HOW TO PLAY

Goal

You've noclipped into an endless office maze. Collect 30 ★ Stars, bring them back to the camp where you started, and step through the Escape Portal.

Earn a star by escaping a chase (break line-of-sight and survive 20 seconds), by killing a monster, or by squashing a monster hiding in a saferoom bed. Carried stars are lost if you die. Walk into camp to bank them.

Day & Night

Day lasts 10 minutes. The lights are on and monsters are rare — they just follow you slowly. When the lights flicker violently, night is coming: the power dies, most rooms go black and monsters roam for 6 minutes.

Monsters

They're blind and hunt by sound. If one gets line of sight at night it chases you — your flashlight cuts out for a second when it spots you. Monsters hit you rather than killing you outright, so you can fight back or run.

The Hollow — tall and eyeless. The Crawler — low, fast, fragile. The Brute — slow, but hits like a truck. The Hound — very fast. The Smiler — night only; your flashlight beam burns it. The Mannequin — can't move while you look at it.

The map

Some rooms are sunken (find the ramp out), some have holes in the floor (ladders lead out), some are flooded. Far from camp you may find the Poolrooms. Falling hurts.

Vents run above the ceiling. Climb a ladder into one to hide — monsters can't see or hear you up there. Crawling wears the vent down (faster if you hurry); stay still and it slowly recovers. It collapses at 0 or after 2 minutes, and monsters sometimes claw at it. Find an exit grate and press E to drop out.

Survival

Health (above your hotbar) drops when monsters hit you or you fall. It slowly comes back a while after you were last hurt — much faster at camp. Bandages and first aid kits heal you.

Sanity drains, faster at night and near monsters; at 0 you die. Hunger drains while you move. Energy drains while moving; stand still to recover.

Loot & crafting

Every drawer of a cabinet can be searched on its own — look at the one you want. They hold batteries, food, drinks, keys, weapons, meds and materials (wood, metal, cloth, tape). Bring materials to the crafting table at camp to make weapons, bandages, batteries, keys and noisemakers. Store spare food and batteries in the chest at camp — it keeps them when you die.

Eat a banana and you keep the peel — throw or drop it so chasers slip. Throw rocks or a noisemaker to lure monsters away.

Phone & tablet

Left thumb: thumbstick. Right side: drag to look. Tap anywhere to punch. Buttons: attack · use · jump · interact · sprint · crouch · light · battery · drop peel · pause. Tap hotbar slots to switch items.
