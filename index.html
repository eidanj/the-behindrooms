HOW TO PLAY

Goal

You've noclipped into an endless office maze. Collect 30 ★ Stars, bring them back to the camp where you started, and step through the Escape Portal.

Earn a star by escaping a chase (break line-of-sight with a monster and survive 20 seconds), by killing a monster, or by squashing a monster hiding in a saferoom bed. Stars you carry are lost if you die. Walk into camp to bank them safely.

Day & Night

Day lasts 10 minutes. The lights are on, the music is calm, and monsters are rare — they just follow you slowly.

When the lights start to flicker violently, night is coming. The power dies, only 1–3 dim lights per room stay on, and monsters roam. Night lasts 6 minutes.

Monsters

They're blind and hunt by sound. Walking can be heard from 5–20 ft, sprinting from 10–35 ft. At night, if one gets line of sight, it sprints at you slightly faster than you walk — so sprint away and break line of sight.

Nearby monsters make lights and your flashlight flicker, drain your sanity and tint your vision red. Listen for heavy breathing.

Your flashlight makes you easier to spot at night. Press L to turn it off.

Survival

Sanity slowly drains, faster at night and near monsters. Below 50 your vision greys and you tire faster; below 30 it darkens; below 10 it flickers. At 0 you die.

Hunger drains while you move (3× while sprinting). Below 35 you can't sprint and your sanity drains.

Energy drains while moving. At 0 you're exhausted and slow. Stand still to slowly recover.

Loot

Search filing cabinets for batteries (5 min each), food, drinks and Saferoom Keys. Saferooms are sealed, monster-proof rooms; some have a bed to sleep through the night. But at night, listen at the door — if you hear breathing and see a bulge under the blanket, don't sleep. Jump on it instead.

Throw rocks to lure monsters away or hit them to trip them. Throw or drop banana peels to make chasers slip.

Fighting

Press F (or hold slot 7 and click) to punch. Fists are weak; drawers can hold a lead pipe, crowbar, baseball bat or fire axe, which hit much harder but break after a while. Every hit knocks the monster back and stuns it for a moment. Monsters that are knocked down by a rock or a peel take double damage. Killing one earns +1 ★. Attacking a monster in daylight makes it hostile.
